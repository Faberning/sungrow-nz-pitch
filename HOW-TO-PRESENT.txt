SUNGROW NZ PITCH PACK - HOW TO PRESENT
Prepared by Eko Media. Private and confidential.

BEFORE THE MEETING
1. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All. Then open the extracted folder.
   Opening files from inside the zip breaks the links, logo and images.
2. Double-click index.html. It opens in Chrome or Edge (use one of those, full screen with F11).
3. Keep every file and folder together. The pages link to each other.
4. No internet is needed. Everything, including the map and both videos, works offline.
5. Play the film once beforehand to check the room's sound.

MOVING AROUND
- Arrow keys, Page Down or Space move one section. The dots on the right jump to any section.
- The buttons in the top bar open the film, the NZ hub mock-up, the installer map, the installer
  dashboard (the national portal for Sungrow installers) and the search baselines.
- Every demo page has a link back to the review.

SUGGESTED RUN (about 15 minutes)
1. Cover - New Zealand asks Google which battery to buy, and Sungrow is not in the answer.
2. The film (84 seconds). Play the 16:9 version. The phone version sits beside it.
3. Sections 01 to 05 - the findings on Google, AI answers, sources, and the Tauhei solar farm story.
4. Section 06 - click around the live NZ hub mock-up inside the slide, or open it full screen.
5. Installer map demo:
   - Type "Tauranga", press Search, then tap "Coastal install".
   - Type "Dunedin" to show the gap: no certified installer within 100 km.
6. Sections 07 and 08 - the questions to own, and what the website tells machines.
7. Section 09 - the five-step plan. Each step with a demo has a button that opens it.
   Step 2 has two: the map homeowners use, and the installer dashboard.
8. Installer dashboard - what Sungrow's certified installers would log in to. Walk through:
   - Overview: leads, notices and training at a glance.
   - Network map: the same national map, with coverage gaps (Dunedin, Invercargill).
   - Communications: product news (MG12RL, 3-phase residential ESS), bulletins, training.
   - Library: search "MG12RL" or "SBR" to show manuals, guides and datasheets.
   - Marketing assets: approved images, the badge and the film for installers to share.
9. Search baselines - today's numbers. The "Day 90" toggle shows where the re-run results will go.
10. Sections 10 and 11 - where we start, and what we need from Sungrow.
11. Next step - make Sungrow the name New Zealanders hear first.

SAY THIS OUT LOUD
- The installer names on the map are samples, not real businesses. The real map needs
  Sungrow's certified NZ installer list.
- Contact details, installer regions and FAQ answers on the hub are placeholders until
  Sungrow confirms them.
- The photos and product banners on the hub and portal come from sungrowpower.com.
- Everything in the installer portal is sample data: leads, notices, cases, training and
  the badge. Library documents are slots for Sungrow to fill with its real files.
- All figures come from Eko's scan on 14 October 2026 (Auckland, logged out).
- Nobody can promise what an AI engine will say. The work makes Sungrow readable,
  trustworthy and present in the sources those engines draw from.
