01 · The Google foundation

On Google, New Zealand has no Sungrow front door.

Google is where AI engines read first. Today a New Zealand buyer who searches for Sungrow, its warranty or a Sungrow installer is sent to Australia, to resellers, or to a competitor.

08 · What the website tells machines

The site is built for Australia. New Zealand is a footnote.

Each site is scored on how easily a machine can reach it, read it, and find who the business is and how to contact it. Sungrow's Australian site scores 65%. Sigenergy's scores 84%.

SiteScoreMain gaps
sungrowpower.com/au/en65%No H1 heading. A 9-character meta description. Structured data does not say who the business is. No direct phone or email link.
en.sungrowpower.com63%No H1 heading. Structured data does not say who the business is. No direct phone or email link.
service.sungrowpower.com.au76%No meta description. Eight H1 headings on one page. A quarter of images described for machines.
sigenergy.com/au84%Images carry no descriptions. Everything else machines need is in place.

No site blocks AI crawlers, which is good. None publishes an llms.txt file. Four further findings were checked by hand:

Website

No /nz route

sungrowpower.com/nz returns 404. New Zealand is served by the Australian regional site, so every page carries Australian context.

Website

No NZ contact data

The service site lists a North Sydney office, an Australian 1800 number and a +61 line. Machines read that as "no local presence".

Website

Installer finder points to distributors

The regional site offers "Find a Distributor". Sigenergy's regional site offers "Find an Installer", which is what a homeowner and an AI answer need.

Website

Warranty pages split across four domains

Google returns warranty pages on service.sungrowpower.com.au, sungrowpower.com (an India path), sungrowpowerservice.com and Australian retailer PDFs.

Scan of each home page, 14 Oct 2026, scoring reachability, structured data, AI files, citable content and contact path. Page speed was not measured this round. Pages fetched live the same day: sungrowpower.com/en, /au/en, /nz; service.sungrowpower.com.au; sigenergy.com/au.
